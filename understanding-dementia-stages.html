The one rule worth printing

A sudden change is medical until proven otherwise. The disease moves slowly. If confusion, agitation, or sleepiness arrives fast — over hours or a day or two — think infection, pain, or a new medication first. That's often delirium, it's frequently reversible, and it needs a phone call, not a behaviour strategy.
