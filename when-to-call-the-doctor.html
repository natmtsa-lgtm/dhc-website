The one rule underneath all three

A sudden change is medical until proven otherwise. The disease moves slowly. If confusion, agitation or sleepiness arrives fast — over hours or a day or two — think infection, pain, constipation or a new medication first. That is often delirium, it is frequently reversible, and it needs a phone call, not a behaviour strategy. If you are unsure, assume it needs assessing and make the call.
