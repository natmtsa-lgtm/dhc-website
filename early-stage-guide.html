Questions families ask

Is it too early to buy this?

If you have a diagnosis, it is not too early — this stage is the one with the shortest window. The paperwork, the driving conversation and the life story all get harder to do well the longer they wait.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is general education for families, written by a physician, and it does not replace your person’s own doctor or care team. Anything that looks like a sudden change, an infection or an injury needs assessing rather than managing — if you are unsure, assume it does and make the call.
